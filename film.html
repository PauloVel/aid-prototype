AID — The film

They said

slow down.

We said

no.

Fear
is loud.

Go
anyway.

Heart
in throat.

Hands
steady.

No net.

No
applause.

No
permission.

Just

the rush.

We live

for it.

AID

turns it

into posters.

Into things

you can

feel.

Not for

everyone.

Only for

the ones

who go
first.

Be

unmistakable.

Brands.

Campaigns.

Screens.

Objects.

All of it

one voice.

Never
a copy.

Never
safe.

Never
quiet.

Be one

of one.

Go
first.
